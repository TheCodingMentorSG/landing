About Us

Coding is the New Literacy

At The Coding Mentor, we believe that coding is a child's playground— a medium for expression, problem-solving, and logic.

Our curriculum is designed specifically for the Singapore context, focusing on developing computational thinking through hands-on project-based learning. This is in line with framework prescribed by the Ministry of Education.

  • ✓ Small Class Sizes (Max 1:3 Ratio)
  • ✓ MOE Registered Instructor
  • ✓ 100% Hands-on Coding from Day 1
