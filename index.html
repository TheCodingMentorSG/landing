Frequently Asked Questions

Isn't 9 too young to code?

+

Absolutely not! 9-year-olds naturally understand sequences and logic through play. This is actually the perfect age to build foundational thinking skills before formal education begins.

What if my child has never seen code before?

+

Perfect! We design everything from scratch for beginners. No prior experience needed. Our visual, interactive approach makes it feel more like a game than a tech class.

Can I stay and watch the class?

+

We ask parents to drop off so children can focus without distractions. But we're happy to chat after class and give you detailed feedback on how your child did!

Where exactly in Singapore?

+

Classes are held at home classroom at tutor's place or at your home for your comfort. We offer flexible arrangements to suit your family's needs.

Can I get a refund if I can't make it?

+

The booking is non-refundable, but you can reschedule for free within a month with 24 hours' notice. If we cancel due to unforeseen circumstances, full refund guaranteed.
