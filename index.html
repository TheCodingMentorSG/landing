Common Questions from Parents

Is 5 really not too young?

Not at all! Our experience shows that students who start at kindergarten age more easily cultivate good problem solving and learning attitudes that help lay the foundation for coding success.

Can my child start earlier than 5?

Research by a group of educators in Singapore has shown that children younger than 5 cannot grasp the concept of loops well. This affects their learning progression. Starting at a minimum of 5 and no younger is best!

Can parents stay and watch?

Parents drop off to help kids focus better. You're welcome to speak to the teacher after class for a face-to-face teacher feedback.
