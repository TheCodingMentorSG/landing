Our Story

From Classmates to Mentors

"We didn't just want to teach code; we wanted to journey and nurture a generation of creative thinkers and innovators!"

01Why We Started

The prologue of this story stems from a shared vision between Polytechnic students after graduation. Equipped with IT skills and a budding passion to teach and inspire, we coined the question: How do we create a platform to inspire learning in Singapore, while still remaining close to our passions in Information Technology? This thinking led us to our answer: The Coding Mentor.

02Our Journey and Goal

In this day and age, technology is the new literacy. Our mission is to delve deep into the world of coding with kids and teens, transforming them from passive consumers of technology into active architects of it. We provide a space where "trial and error" is celebrated and learning is synonymous with play, ensuring students are ready for a digital future.

By bridging the gap between academic theory and practical application, we help our students see code not just as lines on a screen, but as a tool to solve real-world problems.

Learning Roadmap

Course Modules

A structured progression from fundamentals to professional mastery.

Core (Ages 7 - 11)
Module 01

ScratchJr

Visual block coding for younger starters to build stories and animations without typing.

Module 02

Scratch Coding

The sweet spot for this age range. Build real games, platformers, and master complex logic.

Module 03

Python Coding

Transition to popular text-based coding language, Python, used by engineers at NASA, Google, and Netflix.

Pathways (Ages 12+)
04
